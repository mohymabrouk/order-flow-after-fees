# Maker or taker per order (Change 16; frozen models, no retraining)

Cost = execution price vs the arrival mid plus fees, bp of the arrival mid. Saving = benchmark cost − policy cost (positive = the policy is cheaper). Fees: taker 5 bp, maker 2 bp (VIP0). Every action takes effect 100 ms after its decision. Orders: one buy and one sell every 60 s per symbol-day (the overlay's orders), 10,000 USDT each. CI: 95 % daily block bootstrap (5,000 resamples of days). Adverse move = mid move against the order after a passive fill; markout = mid after τ vs the fill price, signed so positive = good for the order.
